Decision: reject unconditional rolling replacement. It trades better lease coverage for excessive send starvation. Even an ideal five-call/21s refresh demand for 64 plus 8 sends/s exceeds 20/s before polling; at 350ms the serial sender's theoretical ceiling is 2.86/s, below offered3/s regardless of tenant count. The fast-wave 64/8/s result relies on checks stretching beyond the nominal due time without sampled expiration, not a safe capacity guarantee. Next candidate: separate budget/admission experiment with explicit deadline margin, reserved service shares and bounded per-tenant send concurrency, compare both readiness and bounded backlog below feasible demand; establish a conservative measured per-bot envelope rather than promise 64.
