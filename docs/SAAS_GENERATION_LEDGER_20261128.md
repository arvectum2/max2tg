# P0.5b / P2.2b — bounded successive pin generations

Base e0ff4a3 (PR #71). GenerationLedger is an independent owner-private, exclusively locked journal with exact namespace binding, initial reviewed bundle, fixed kernel object handles and at most 64 sequential rotations. Every grant records its ordered generation, nonce and reviewed bundle before execution; IN_FLIGHT is persisted before mutation and KERNEL_VERIFIED before the receipt. A separate trusted broker adoption acknowledgement (exact nonce, digest, ready=True) plus fresh kernel verification is required before ADOPTED and the next grant. Historical role ownership is checked against every prior generation, including removed addresses. Old grants cannot replay, gaps and mismatches fail closed. Journal readback is bounded; reopened controller is observation-only, even after ADOPTED. Same-address renewal may match more than one observed generation; kernel match is not independent broker adoption proof. Caller must own the namespace and broker and retain journal independently of restored state.
